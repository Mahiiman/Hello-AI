HELLO AI — Privacy Notice

Draft notice · Last updated: November 16, 2026

This notice explains, in general terms, how information may be processed when you use HELLO AI. It must be reviewed and updated to match the actual backend and service-provider settings before public release.

Information you provide

Questions, prompts, images or other content you submit may be sent to the app's backend to provide AI features. Do not submit passwords, financial details or other sensitive personal information.

Voice features

If you use voice input or speech features, audio or recognized speech may be processed by your device or the speech services configured in the app. Text-to-speech requests may be sent to the configured speech provider.

Service providers

The app code uses Supabase services and AI and speech endpoints to provide features. Information sent to these services may be processed under their respective terms and privacy practices. The exact data retention, logging and deletion settings have not yet been independently confirmed.

Data security and retention

Reasonable safeguards should be maintained for the app and its connected services. How long submitted content or technical logs are retained depends on the configured backend and service providers. This notice does not promise that data is never stored or that it is automatically deleted.

Children

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use. Do not knowingly submit a child's sensitive personal information.

Contact and questions

For questions or support, use the HELLO AI GitHub support page. Avoid posting private or sensitive information in public issues.

Important: The developer must verify backend logging, AI-provider processing, speech-provider processing, retention and contact details, then revise this draft before treating it as the final privacy policy.
