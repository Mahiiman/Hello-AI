HELLO AI — Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This notice explains how HELLO AI processes information when you use its chat, translation, writing, image generation, voice, and video-subtitle features. Please read it before submitting personal or confidential information.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, HELLO AI may process text messages, questions, prompts, translation and writing requests, image-generation prompts, recognized speech text, narration text, and video or audio content selected for subtitle generation.

Technical information may also be processed by the app, its hosting services, or service providers to operate and protect the service.

2. AI Chat, Translation, and Writing

When you use AI chat, translation, or writing features, the relevant text is sent to HELLO AI's Supabase backend function and processed using Google Gemini.

The reviewed backend code sets the request option store: false. This describes the request configuration; it is not a guarantee that Google, network providers, or infrastructure providers retain no technical records or data.

3. Image Generation

When you request an image, your prompt is sent to a HELLO AI backend function. The function uses AI Horde and may also use Google Gemini to improve the prompt when configured.

Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personal information in image prompts. The privacy and retention practices of these external providers are governed by their own policies.

4. Voice Input and Speech

Voice input uses speech-recognition features provided by the browser or device. The reviewed app code processes the resulting recognized text as a message and does not explicitly upload the raw microphone recording to the HELLO AI backend in this flow.

However, the browser, operating system, or speech-recognition provider may process voice data under its own privacy terms. Basic spoken playback of AI replies uses browser or device speech-synthesis functionality.

5. AI Narration

If you use AI narration, the text and selected voice information are sent to the external TTS.ai service. HELLO AI then checks the narration job and retrieves the resulting audio.

Please avoid using this feature with confidential information or personal data that you do not want shared with an external service provider.

6. Video Subtitles

The video-subtitle feature uses FFmpeg and a Whisper Tiny speech-recognition model. The model is downloaded from Hugging Face. The reviewed app code processes the selected media and performs transcription in the browser after the model is loaded.

Downloading model files and using browser-based processing may involve network requests and technical information handled by the browser, hosting services, or model provider.

7. Supabase and Account Information

HELLO AI uses Supabase for backend services and anonymous authentication. You may receive an anonymous authentication session when using the app.

At the time of the backend review on November 16, 2026, no app-specific public database tables or files in Supabase Storage were found. Anonymous authentication records and session-related technical records were present.

This review does not establish that no server logs, security records, backups, or other technical records exist. Their complete retention periods and deletion procedures have not been verified.

8. Service Providers

Depending on the features you use, HELLO AI may rely on the following services:

  • Supabase — backend services and anonymous authentication.
  • Google Gemini — AI chat, translation, writing, and possible image-prompt improvement.
  • AI Horde — image generation.
  • TTS.ai — AI narration.
  • Hugging Face — hosting model files used for video subtitles.
  • Browser and device providers — speech recognition and speech synthesis.

These providers may process information under their own privacy notices, terms, and retention practices. HELLO AI does not control all of their data-handling practices.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

HELLO AI does not promise that all information is automatically deleted immediately after use. Data may remain in provider systems, technical logs, security records, or backups according to the relevant provider's policies and retention settings.

A complete deletion process covering HELLO AI infrastructure and every external provider has not been verified. Contact the developer through the support link below for questions or requests. A request to HELLO AI cannot guarantee deletion of copies independently held by external providers.

10. Security

Reasonable technical measures are intended to protect the service. However, no internet transmission, application, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Avoid submitting passwords, financial details, identity documents, or other highly sensitive information.

11. Children

HELLO AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission where required by applicable law. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact the developer.

12. Changes to This Notice

This notice may be updated when the app, its features, or its service providers change. The latest version will be published on the HELLO AI website with an updated date.

13. Contact and Support

For privacy questions or support, contact the developer through the HELLO AI GitHub support page.
